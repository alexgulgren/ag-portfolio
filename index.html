How I work

Principles I keep coming back to

From the platform redesign

Fix the system, not the symptom

When users were building their own workarounds, patching individual screens wasn't going to help. I pitched a system-level redesign of navigation, terminology and components so the product could scale.

From the AI Assistant

Earn trust before you automate

Full AI scoring was fast, but users didn't trust it. We chose a hybrid where instructors stay in the loop and transcripts show where every piece of feedback came from.

From product-led growth

Design for who can actually buy

An admin, a licensed instructor and a student need different upgrade paths. I mapped permissions by role first, then decided where signage and purchasing should appear.

From the design system work

Leave the team faster than you found it

Good structure is a product for designers. Templates with libraries preloaded, clear folders and one source of truth help a new designer get productive in under an hour.
