Turning a sales-led product into a self-serve one

GoReact's growth was capped by a sales-led model. I designed permission-aware upgrade paths, in-product signage and a free trial so users could discover value and buy on their own. The Advanced package brought in $400K+ in 2025.

Context

The problem

Relying on sales meant high acquisition costs, weak trial-to-paid conversion, and friction for anyone who wanted to try the product on their own. After a successful beta of the Advanced features, we found the product was missing:

  • Intuitive upgrade paths inside the app
  • Clear feature promotion and plan signage
  • Self-serve trial and upgrade flows
  • A way to upgrade without involving sales

Key decision

Design around permissions

GoReact's customers buy in very different ways. A university admin, an instructor with their own license and a student can't all have the same "Upgrade" button. Before designing screens, I mapped who can buy, who can request, and where each person should see signage.

User typeCan upgradeWhere they see signage
AdminAll licenses in the orgDashboard, feedback panel, activity editor
Instructor with a licenseTheir own licenseDashboard, feedback panel, activity editor
Enterprise instructor with a licenseCan request from an adminFeedback panel, activity editor
Instructor without a licenseCan request from an adminFeedback panel, activity editor
StudentNoFeedback panel

This turned into permission-based workflows: show or hide upgrade signage by role, add purchase and license-request flows, and place each entry point where that user already sees value.

Solution

What we shipped

Advanced package signage. Feature badges, tease walls for advanced features, and free trial signage with its own workflow.

Self-serve purchasing. Purchase and upgrade signage on the dashboard for license holders, end-to-end purchase and upgrade flows, and license management that shows people what they have.

Sales still supported enterprise customers, while expansion and feature adoption moved into the product.
