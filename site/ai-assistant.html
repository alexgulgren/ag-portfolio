Understanding & Defining the Problem

Given the complex workflows and multiple user roles within GoReact, instructors face the challenge of providing timely and effective feedback, which can be time-consuming and overwhelming. This initiative focused on designing a solution that streamlines the feedback process, reduces cognitive load for instructors, and enables higher-quality, actionable insights for learners.

Business Problems

Instructor inefficiency

The review workload exceeded what a single instructor could realistically handle efficiently

Delayed time to value
Feedback gated by instructor availability, slowing learning momentum

Value Proposition

Both learner and instructor value was dependent on user effort and time
