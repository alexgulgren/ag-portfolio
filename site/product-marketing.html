What is GoReact?

GoReact is a skill mastery platform that enables learners to improve performance-based skills through video submissions and structured feedback. Instructors and reviewers provide actionable insights, while the platform supports iterative learning across diverse workflows and user roles.

Understanding & Defining the Problem

Users struggled to quickly understand and adopt new GoReact features. Messaging and guidance were often unclear or overly technical, leading to low engagement and adoption. This initiative aimed to clarify feature value, simplify communication, and help users get immediate value from new releases.

Unclear feature value
Users lacked quick understanding of what new features did and why they mattered

Social media workflow gap
Marketing posts lacked alignment with actual product workflows, limiting relevance

Low social engagement
Posts were static and text-heavy, reducing user interaction and reach
