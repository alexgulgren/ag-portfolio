Understanding & Defining the Problem

Kohler Energy Management customers rely on cellular subscriptions to keep their generators connected to the app. However, the current system does not allow customers to renew early without sacrificing their remaining subscription data.

Key Problems

Homeowner Impact
Homeowners must wait until their current subscription expires before renewing, often while continuing to see low-data warnings. This creates uncertainty around when and how they should renew.

Dealer Impact

Dealers must manually track individual expiration dates outside of the application and communicate the correct renewal timing to each homeowner, adding unnecessary administrative work and increasing the opportunity for missed or delayed renewals.

Opportunity

Create a more seamless renewal experience that allows customers to purchase their next subscription in advance while preserving their existing data—reducing manual dealer involvement and giving homeowners confidence that their connectivity will continue uninterrupted.

Current Experiences

Research Summary

To understand the renewal experience from both the user and technical perspectives, we combined direct dealer research with cross-functional discovery.

  • Mapped user journeys to better understand gaps and areas of improvement

  • Conducted dealer interviews to understand subscription renewal challenges, existing workflows, and common pain points

  • Collaborated with the Partner HQ Product Manager and engineering team to determine where the new functionality should live (KEM apps or Partner HQ)

  • Identified the API connections and data requirements needed to support subscription stacking across platforms

  • Defined technical and platform limitations that needed to be considered during design.

Solution Exploration

Assumptions proven wrong:

Auto-renewal workflow should start and mainly live in Partner HQ

Partner HQ stores payment information and customer data

Homeowners can purchase their own cell kit subscriptions

Outcomes:

The bulk of the auto-renewal workflow will live in KEM and make calls to partner HQ for payment which then makes a call to salesforce

We store customer data but will not store payment information or take payments in app

Homeowners must contact dealer to make a payment
