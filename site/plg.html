Understanding & Defining the Problem

GoReact’s growth was constrained by a sales-led conversion model and limited self-service capabilities. This created high acquisition costs, poor trial-to-paid conversion, and friction for users who wanted to experience value on their own.

After a successful beta of the Advanced features, it was clear that the product lacked:

Intuitive upgrade paths inside the app

Clear feature promotion and plan signage

Self-serve trial and upgrade flows

Autonomous upgrade experiences without sales involvemen
