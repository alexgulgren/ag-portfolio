Understanding & Defining the Problem

The first step in the Kohler Energy Management homeowner app is connecting a generator. While the process appears straightforward, interacting with the physical hardware introduces complexity that creates friction for users.

Key Problems

Controller PIN

Steps to find the PIN are technical and complicated

High support burden

Generator connection is one of the top three issues driving support requests, indicating a breakdown in the onboarding flow.

Cognitive overload

Users are presented with multiple external resources, pulling them out of the app and increasing confusion during a critical setup step.

Manual Processes

Manual input of serial numbers is required– even though we can connect the device with the customers login credentials.
