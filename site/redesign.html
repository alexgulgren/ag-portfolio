Understanding & Defining the Problem

When I joined the company, the product had an outdated design system and fragmented information architecture that created inconsistencies across core workflows and made the experience difficult to scale. Rather than addressing issues individually, I proposed a system-level redesign focused on improving end-to-end user journeys, accessibility, and long-term product scalability.

Business Problems

Scaling Limitations
Poor information architecture limiting scalability for future features

Cognitive Load & Product Trust

Busy & inconsistent product experience across modules due to absence of a design system

Design system lacked documentation and governance
Very minimal design system documentation creating inconsistencies and difficult onboarding
